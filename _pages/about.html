A little about me.

Hey 👋! I’m a second-year Mechanical Engineering student at the University of Toronto, pursuing minors in Engineering Business and Robotics & Mechatronics. While mechanical engineering is my home, I’m also interested in software development and machine learning. I like understanding how things work, whether that means taking apart stuff, working through code, or following a question down a Google search rabbit hole. Welcome to my digital garden!

My background ranges from entrepreneurship to teaching and engineering design. Before university, I held leadership roles in the Royal Canadian Air Cadets, coordinating a band of over 30 cadets, captaining a marksmanship team, and coaching more than 100 cadets. Those experiences taught me effective organization, communication, and adapt how to my approach to different people. I brought these lessons into running my own private swimming instruction service, supported by my National Lifeguard, Swim Instructor, and Standard First Aid with CPR-C qualifications. Through more than 100 personalized lessons, I helped students build confidence in the water while managing scheduling, payments, and parent communication.

This mix of hands-on work and working with people shapes how I approach engineering: understand the problem, build something, and keep improving it. I use SOLIDWORKS for CAD and mechanical design, JavaScript/TypeScript, HTML, and SCSS for front-end development, and Python and C++ for simulation and software development. At University of Toronto Supermileage, I’m developing a rear shelf assembly to accommodate the motor, battery, and drivetrain mounts, with plans to learn ANSYS for finite element analysis as the design progresses. I also contribute to Blue Sky Solar Racing’s Strategy Team, reviewing and helping improve C++ race-planning software that supports energy management and strategy decisions.

Outside school, I’m usually playing piano, binge-reading whatever catches my attention, or finding a reason to 'touch grass'. Coming from a background in swimming and cycling, I decided that in 2026 summer I was going to combine them, adding on a run, and do my first triathlon! I also enjoy exploring the city, finding a trail, or doing whatever outdoors to kill time. There’s usually always another side quest waiting to be done.
